# Background music (copyright-free)
Yahan apni royalty-free music files daalo (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`).
- Pixabay Music: https://pixabay.com/music/ (free license, download karke yahan rakho)
- YouTube Audio Library: https://studio.youtube.com → Audio Library

App har video me inme se ek track lagata hai aur voice aate hi music apne aap dheema (ducking) ho jata hai.
Dusron ke gaane / Instagram audio yahan mat daalo — copyright strike aa sakti hai.
